# superstore-sales-analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Superstore Orders dataset to identify sales trends, profit performance, and the impact of discounts on revenue and profitability.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Seaborn
- Excel
- MySQL
- Numpy
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Power BI

## Analysis Performed
1. Sales by Category
2. Profit by Category
3. Sales by Segment
4. Monthly Sales Trend
5. Profit by Sub-Category
6. Discount vs Profit
7. Discount vs Sales
8. Top 10 Products by Sales
9. Profit by Region
10. Correlation Heatmap

## Key Insights
- Technology category generated the highest sales.
- Some sub-categories generate high sales but low profit.
- Higher discounts often lead to lower profit.
- Sales show fluctuations across months indicating seasonal demand.

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights important trends in sales performance and profitability. It also shows how discounts influence revenue and profit margins, helping businesses make better pricing and sales decisions.
